`spec-not-in-claims`: a section walks a type's members when three or more distinct public members are named under the type's heading by code-span labels such as `` `history.undo()` ``, `x.member(` calls on any receiver, or `Type.member` text; that both joins the type and documents its surface (lively's `HistoryManager` / `Room` sections got their gaps back). Option keys do not count. A receiver the fence declares (`const history = doc.getHistory()`) is no longer mistaken for the browser global of that name.
